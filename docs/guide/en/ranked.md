## Actions and star ratings

Exceeding PAR does not fail the stage; you may still finish. Each board has two optional runes, drawn as circular frames with a cross inside.

| Rating | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 star | Clear above PAR, regardless of runes. |
| 2 stars | Clear within PAR without collecting both runes. |
| 3 stars | Clear within PAR and collect both runes. |

PAR is the shortest clear that collects both runes. Every stage also has a shorter route that leaves at least one rune behind. Beyond 255 actions, MOV displays 255+ and the rating is one star.

## The 40-stage campaign and retrying

<!-- par-table -->

F opens stage selection. Choose with WASD or the pad, then press RETURN or the button. All 40 stages are available from the start; you can inspect the selected stage's PAR and every stage's best rating. SPACE from stage selection returns to the title.

After a clear, SPACE retries the same stage and RETURN advances. BEST is your highest rating; NOW is this attempt's rating. A lower-rated clear never reduces BEST. To continue after ending the game, record your password.

## Continue with a password

Write down **PW** at the bottom of the title or stage-selection screen. It restores the selected stage and the highest ratings for all 40 stages. It does not save a partially played board; press RETURN after restoring to start that stage afresh.

Codes contain **4–24 letters**, excluding spaces; progressing through consecutive three-star clears usually needs 5–6. They use no digits, only these 16 uppercase letters:

```text
ACDEFGHJKMNPQRTW
```

1. Press X at the title or stage-selection screen.
2. Enter the code and press RETURN to restore. Separator spaces may be entered or omitted.
3. Correct with Backspace (the minus key on real hardware); cancel with X.

You may also select letters with pad directions and add them with the button. Select LOAD and press the button to restore. DEL removes one letter; BACK cancels. Invalid codes and codes from another game are rejected without changing your current progress.
