Choose between two cards and drop them onto a 4×4 orbital board. Read the next three cards and build scores through row/column rotations and falling chains. The deal changes with your starting timing. Board state and score carry forward through as many rounds as you can survive.

## Controls and play

1. Choose one of the two candidates at the upper right with A/D, then confirm with RETURN.
2. Choose a column with A/D and press RETURN to drop into the empty slot between the arrows. The unused candidate stays; only the used slot is refilled from the left end of NEXT. NEXT previews three cards, from left to right.
3. Move below the board with S to select CARD, ROW, or COL. S during candidate selection goes directly to this menu.
4. In ROW mode, W/S selects a row and RETURN rotates it one cell right. In COL mode, A/D selects a column and RETURN rotates it one cell down. Cards at the edge wrap to the opposite side.
5. ROW and COL remain selected after rotation. To resume placing cards, explicitly choose CARD in the menu and press RETURN.

Cards visibly fall into empty slots. Rotations show their intermediate positions and wraparound. A match glows through three expanding stages before disappearing with sound. Falling cards that match again form a chain, with a new sound, extra points, and restored rotations. Wait for each animation before pressing again.

A is a star, B a ringed planet, C a moon, D a comet, and E a satellite. The game begins with four different cards along the bottom row.

Three or more identical bodies in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line disappear. A line of four counts as two overlapping triples; intersecting triples count separately, but a card is never scored twice in the same clear. Matches are checked immediately after a rotation, then gaps are filled downward; further matches create chains.

| Display | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| SCORE | Total score, capped at 9999 |
| CHAIN X | Chain count from the last action |
| GOAL | Points this round / target; points displayed up to 99 |
| SPIN | Remaining rotations, up to 4 |
| RND | Round number; play continues with the display held at 255 thereafter |

Each clear scores: cards removed × 2 × chain count, plus 2 for each simultaneous triple after the first. One triple earns 6 points, a line of four earns 10, and a triple on the second chain earns 12.

You start with two rotations. Each clear restores one, up to four. Rotating a row/column containing only identical cards or only empty slots costs nothing. A rotation that merely makes cards fall still costs one.

The first target is 12 points. On reaching it, a jingle and result appear; RETURN continues. Targets rise by 6 to 60 and play continues beyond that. The board, candidates, previews, total score, and remaining rotations carry over; only round points reset. Excess points count toward the total but not the next round's target.

A full 16-cell board remains playable while rotations remain. A full board without rotations ends the game with FULL BOARD - NO SPINS. RETURN or SPACE opens the new-game confirmation.

Leave room for upcoming cards. Simply assigning one column per type will not fit five types into four columns. Placing a different body above a group you plan to clear can create horizontal or diagonal chains after it falls. Rotations also move empty slots, so plan their use before filling the board.
