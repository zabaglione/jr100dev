Defeat moving formations of 18 ships across six waves. Armored ships have 2 HP; later waves add armored rows and more shots aimed at your ship.

## Controls and play

A/D moves horizontally. RETURN fires a normal shot; W fires a heavy shot. Normal shots deal 1 damage and add 2 heat; heavy shots deal 2 damage and add 4 heat. Heat caps at 8 and dissipates over time.

Stars, normal shots, thick heavy shots, and enemy bullets fill the scene. Armor breaking has its own flash, distinct from the light and debris of a destroyed ship. Overheating and taking damage also have warning sounds. Wait for animations to finish before pressing again.

HULL is health, ENEMIES counts ships remaining, and HEAT is firing heat. Choose between destroying armor with one heavy shot or keeping heat low to sustain fire. Zero hull or an escaped formation causes failure.
