Pull metal crates toward you with magnetism instead of pushing them, and fill two diamond-shaped sockets. Across 40 stages, crate, socket, and starting positions vary; later rooms require planning around narrow corridors and crate order. Collect the optional circular runes yourself while transporting the crates.

## Controls and play

WASD walks and changes facing. RETURN pulls the crate directly ahead one cell toward you while you step backward. A move, a change of facing, or a successful pull each counts as one action. Turning in place toward a wall or crate also counts. Input that changes neither position nor facing, and failed pulls, do not count. Crates cannot collect runes.

The robot faces its movement direction. Walking and pulling show intermediate positions and movement sounds, making the affected crate clear. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

PULLS under MAGNET counts successful pulls; FACING is N/S/W/E. MOV counts actions, PAR is the target, and RUNES counts optional runes collected.
