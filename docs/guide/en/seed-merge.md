Combine equal-numbered seeds on a 4×4 board to make 64. A new 2 appears whenever the board moves. Running out of legal moves ends the attempt.

## Controls and play

WASD slides the whole board. Each pair of equal seeds can merge only once during a single action.

<!-- common-controls -->

Seed movement, merging, closing gaps, and the new seed's appearance are shown in sequence. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

The display shows each cell's number, the highest value, and the number of moves.
