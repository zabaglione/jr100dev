Jump over neighboring stones to remove them and reduce the garden to five stones or fewer.

## Controls and play

WASD selects a cell. Press RETURN to select a stone, move to an empty cell two spaces away, then press RETURN again. The stone between the starting and landing cells is removed.

<!-- common-controls -->

The selected stone visibly jumps over its neighbor and lands in the empty hole. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

The display shows the garden, selected stone, and number remaining.
