Survive six nights by dodging in eight directions while close-range automatic fire and an area pulse hold back the swarm. Required kills rise from 12 to 22, and later waves spawn more quickly. Armored enemies need two hits.

## Controls and play

Move in eight directions with QWE / AD / ZXC. RETURN fires a pulse reaching up to three cells around you, with an eight-count recharge. A circular portal previews the next enemy entrance. Every four kills drop salvage; step on it to restore 1 hull and recharge the pulse immediately.

A double ring marks the next spawn. Automatic shots, intermediate enemy movement, and an expanding pulse make attacks visible. Armor flashes when hit; defeated enemies turn from a flash into debris. Salvage collection and damage use different sounds. Wait for animations to finish before pressing again.

HULL is health, DOWN is kills/target, PULSE is the remaining recharge, and SALVAGE counts pickups. Maximum hull is 4. Choose whether to maintain a safe distance or move into danger for a supply that lets you keep attacking.
