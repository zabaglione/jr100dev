Exchange cards in a five-card hand and reach 25 total points across three scoring rounds. No pair earns 3 points, one pair 8, multiple matching pairs 16, and five cards of one suit 25.

## Controls and play

Move the arrow above the cards with A/D. W exchanges that card, up to twice per hand. RETURN scores the hand and deals the next one.

<!-- common-controls -->

An exchanged card narrows to its edge, then opens to reveal the new face. When scored, the contributing cards flash. The hand name, earned points, and total remain visible with sound and a pause of about two seconds before the next hand. Wait until the animation ends before pressing again.

Each framed card shows its rank, 1–13, and a spade, heart, diamond, or club. Ranks use at most two digits, without a leading zero. A `*` marks each card contributing to the hand. The current hand name and points appear below the cards. NO PAIR and ONE PAIR mean exactly that; TWO PAIRS means two pairs. THREE / FOUR / FIVE OF A KIND identify three to five matching ranks. FULL HOUSE is a triple plus a pair. FLUSH is five cards of the same suit. Hands with multiple equal-rank pair combinations all earn 16 points; a flush takes precedence at 25. SCORE is the total, GOAL the target, and REDRAWS the exchanges remaining.
