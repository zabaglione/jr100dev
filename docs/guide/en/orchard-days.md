Grow crops in a 4×4 orchard over three seasons. Reach harvest scores of 18, 24, and 30 within 28 days. Fast berries need two growth steps and earn 3 points; slow apples need four and earn 7.

## Controls and play

WASD selects a plot or a tool below the field; RETURN works. BERRY / APPLE selects what to plant. Acting on an empty plot plants, a growing plot waters, and a ripe plot harvests. WAIT spends one day waiting; WELL spends one day restoring 3 water. Selecting a crop type takes no time.

The field and instruments have clear borders. Seeds and drops fly to their targets, and rain or watering visibly grows affected plants one step at a time. Harvests move toward the score with sound. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

Start with 8 seeds and 6 water. Planting uses 1 seed; watering uses 1 water; harvesting returns 1 seed. Rain occurs every 4, 5, or 6 days depending on the season, growing every crop one step and adding 4 water. Water caps at 9. Use RAIN IN to decide which crops to plant and when to visit the well.
