Rotate six mirrors to guide incoming light from the left to the receiver at the upper right. The light path updates whenever a mirror turns.

## Controls and play

WASD selects a mirror position; RETURN switches between `/` and the opposite mirror orientation. The intersection of the arrows above and to the right of the board marks your selection.

<!-- common-controls -->

After a rotation, the light extends as a continuous line and bends at mirror faces. Reflection sounds let you follow it toward the receiver. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

The path remains an unbroken line, turning at mirrors. The rotation count appears beside the board.
