Raise and lower an entire row or column of bridges to connect the lower-left bank to the upper-right gate. Solve three different bridge layouts.

## Controls and play

W/S selects a row or column, A/D switches between rows and columns, and RETURN toggles it. The whole selected line is inverted, with arrows at both ends marking its extent. The character and gate remain visible above it. You may make 30 changes per stage.

<!-- common-controls -->

Once a route connects, the character faces the direction of travel and walks through intermediate positions to the gate. On arrival, they raise their arms and jump twice, with a celebration sound followed by the clear jingle. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

SELECT shows ROW or COL and a number from 1–6. CHANGES counts toggles. The stage clears after the character reaches the gate and celebrates.
