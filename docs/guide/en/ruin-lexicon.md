Assign the numbers 1–4 once each to A–D using four inscriptions and their relationships. Each of the 20 puzzles has one unique answer.

## Controls and play

A/D selects a letter, W/S changes its number, and RETURN checks the inscriptions. You have five attempts. An assignment with duplicated numbers cannot be correct.

<!-- common-controls -->

Inscriptions sit within stone frames. Numbers turn through intermediate shapes with sound. Conditions are checked individually, leaving their results in place. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

Use the A+B, B+C, and C+D sums on the left, plus the greater/less relationship between A and D. After a test, a star marks a satisfied condition and X marks a failed one.
